# Aabshar Sanitary — website design

Design prototypes for Aabshar, a sanitary-fittings and plastic-products manufacturer in Gujranwala, Pakistan. The site sells single pieces to homes and cartons to dealer shops.

## Files

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `blueprint.html` | Research, sitemap and build phases |
| `aabshar-v2.html` … `aabshar-v6.html` | Earlier homepage iterations |
| `aabshar-v7.html` | Current design: homepage, product page, checkout and dealer portal |
| `img/` | Photos (Unsplash, free licence) and the logo mark (`logo-mark.svg`) |

## Viewing

Open `aabshar-v7.html` in a browser. The screens are reached by hash links:

- `#/p/AB-1204` — product page (any SKU works)
- `#/checkout` — checkout
- `#/dealer` — dealer portal (use "See the demo portal")

The language menu in the top strip switches between English and Urdu (right to left).

## Notes

- Product names, prices, dealer names and phone numbers are sample data.
- Checkout and payments are a demo: nothing is sent or charged.
- Photos are placeholders until the company's own product shoot.
